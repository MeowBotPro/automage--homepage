# I01 findings — public hierarchy

Status: synthesized exploratory evidence. Isolation: `best_effort`. Three fresh role sessions completed with browser teardown. No real contact submission, account creation or external business action occurred.

## R1 — manufacturing CEO from homepage

At five seconds the participant noticed “组织的 AI 协作与执行层” and the view-how-it-works entry. After reading, they described AutoMage as an AI collaboration/execution layer that organizes WMS/ERP/MES/SRM/chat facts into comparable proposals, keeps human authorization explicit and records execution/receipt evidence. They called the supply-chain page a mechanism demo and asked about real adapters, permissions, deployment, cost and execution boundaries. After an unrelated dinner question and a roughly five-minute pause, they still described the product, example and simulated boundary correctly.

## R2 — partner from Market

The participant entered the current Market candidate, reviewed the parent context and then explored a sales qualification detail. They described the output as structured inquiry information, approved-knowledge answers and a CRM handoff next step. Delayed recall described the page as an AutoMage public capability detail and the Skill as a sales collaboration capability. The participant asked about production adapters, permissions, records, deployment and monitoring. The run satisfies the directional parent-layer observation, but its initial exploration included a move to product/scenario context; it is not a standalone measure of a five-second Market-only memory threshold.

Evidence: `/tmp/automage-hierarchy-market-I01/` (`step-01-5s.png`, `step-02-product-overview.png`, `step-07-boundary.png`, `run-verdict.json`).

## R3 — service CEO from detail

The participant entered the customer churn detail, read the report structure and compared churn review, cancellation/failed-payment retention and broader customer-success strategy. Immediate and delayed recall both described the hierarchy as AutoMage product/brand → public capability library → CRM churn-autopsy Skill. They prepared a two-stage consultation intent without submitting contact information. Remaining questions concerned CRM permissions, integration and batch/report timing.

Evidence: `/tmp/automage-hierarchy-detail-I01/` (`initial-5s.png`, `capability-detail.png`, `consultation-list.png`, `run-verdict.json`).

## Decision

The current product-first homepage, quieter Market and persistent parent cue are directionally supported. The direct Market/detail parent relationship is supported by the supplemental study `public-hierarchy-direct-20260915` (two fresh sessions). The plan's aggregate “2/3 homepage entrants” threshold is not certified because this iteration had one homepage-first role; a release-grade claim requires a separate homepage cohort. No further Market feature or catalog-content expansion is justified by these runs.
