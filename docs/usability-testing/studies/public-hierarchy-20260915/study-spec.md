# Public hierarchy RCUT — I01

Status: frozen for exploratory validation.

Question: does the revised public hierarchy make AutoMage the remembered product, with the scenario as proof and the Skill Market as a capability layer?

The three roles use different entry surfaces. Their prompts contain only a realistic business context and goal. The coordinator records 5-second first impression, immediate post-task recall, and a five-minute delayed recall after an unrelated short task. The acceptance gate is semantic, not exact wording: at least 2/3 homepage entrants should name the product category before mentioning Skills; every direct-entry participant should identify the parent product and current capability-layer status; at least 2/3 should transfer the mechanism to a non-supply-chain problem.

Isolation is best_effort. No real contact submission, Skill execution, account creation or external business write is allowed. This study supplies discovery evidence only and cannot label the product Production-ready.
