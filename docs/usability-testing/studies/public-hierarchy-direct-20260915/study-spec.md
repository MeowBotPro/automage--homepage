# Direct-entry hierarchy RCUT

Status: frozen for exploratory validation. This supplemental study tests the unresolved direct-entry question from the previous hierarchy run. It does not replace or overwrite the earlier evidence.

Two fresh anonymous participants start on public capability surfaces. Before leaving the starting page, each gives a five-second first impression without being told the expected product/category words. After the task, each gives immediate and five-minute delayed recall. The study checks whether the parent product and capability-layer relationship is understood without a homepage detour.

Isolation is best_effort. No real contact submission, Skill execution, account creation or external business write is allowed. This study is discovery evidence, not a Production-ready certification.
